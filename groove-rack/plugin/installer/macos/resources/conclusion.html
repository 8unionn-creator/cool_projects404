Groove Rack is installed

  • Logic Pro: open Logic, make a Software Instrument track and pick AU Instruments → cool_projects404 → Groove Rack.
  • GarageBand: on a Software Instrument track, pick Plug-ins → AU Instruments → cool_projects404 → Groove Rack.
  • Other DAWs: rescan plugins and load Groove Rack as an instrument (VST3).
  • Without a DAW: open Groove Rack from Applications.
