Liftoff

Riser, reverse reverb and pre-drop cut in one effect, locked to your bars.

This installs:

  • Audio Unit for Logic Pro and GarageBand
  • VST3 for Ableton Live, FL Studio, Studio One, Reaper, Cubase and Bitwig
  • the Liftoff app, to run audio from your interface through it without a DAW

Close your DAW before you continue.
