Liftoff is installed

  • Logic Pro: on any track or bus, click an Audio FX slot and pick Audio Units → cool_projects404 → Liftoff.
  • GarageBand: in a track's Plug-ins, pick Audio Units → cool_projects404 → Liftoff.
  • Other DAWs: rescan plugins and put Liftoff (VST3) on a track, a bus or the master.

Liftoff reports its backverb length as latency; your DAW lines everything up by itself.
