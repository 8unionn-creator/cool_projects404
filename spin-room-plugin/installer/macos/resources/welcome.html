Spin Room

Put it on your Master channel and play your beat: Spin Room catches it as it plays and turns it into a video for TikTok, Reels or YouTube.

This installs:

  • Audio Unit for Logic Pro and GarageBand
  • VST3 for FL Studio, Ableton Live, Studio One, Reaper, Cubase and Bitwig
  • the Spin Room app, to catch a beat from an audio input without a DAW

Close your DAW before you continue.
