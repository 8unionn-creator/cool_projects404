Spin Room is installed

  • FL Studio: open Options → Manage plugins → Find plugins, then put Spin Room in the last slot of the Master mixer track.
  • Logic Pro: on the Stereo Out channel, click an Audio FX slot and pick Audio Units → cool_projects404 → Spin Room.
  • Other DAWs: rescan plugins and put Spin Room (VST3) last on the master.

Open it and play your beat from the top. Each run is caught and added to your beats. Videos are saved to Movies → Spin Room.
